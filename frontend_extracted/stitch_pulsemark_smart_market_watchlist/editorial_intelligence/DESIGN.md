## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a warm, authoritative visual environment bridging institutional financial prestige with high-craft editorial journalism. Rather than relying on cold terminal aesthetics, harsh pitch-black backgrounds, or synthetic neon gradients, it articulates rigor through tailored proportions, tactile alabaster surfaces, deep navy structural architecture, and rich saddle-bronze accents.

The design movement synthesizes **Modern Editorial Elegance** with **Institutional Financial Utility**:
- **Human-Crafted Editorial Prestige:** Evoking the tactile permanence of archival financial reviews, bespoke annual briefings, and sovereign wealth reports.
- **Disciplined Precision:** Information architecture designed for high analytical density without cognitive strain, utilizing razor-thin hairline borders, structured typographic hierarchy, and deliberate warm breathing room.
- **Restrained Materiality:** Natural light surfaces, quiet warm-gray divisions, and deliberate chestnut-leather trajectory markers replace ephemeral drop-shadows and fluorescent glowing effects.

## Colors

The color palette centers on a light, warm foundation engineered for prolonged analytical reading, portfolio evaluation, and executive decision-making. 

### Core Palette Mechanics
- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Tailored midnight navy deployed for structural headers, authoritative brand signatures, high-contrast buttons, and heavy metric readouts.
- **Secondary (`#8D5B4C`)**: A warm saddle chestnut applied to analytical accents, executive subheadings, curated insight chips, and thematic markers.
- **Tertiary (`#B45309`)**: Polished bronze amber reserved for directional trajectories, high-importance telemetry, active benchmarks, and focused badges.
- **Neutral (`#F4F5F8`)**: Soft alabaster surface gray with subtle warm undertones, eliminating ocular glare associated with pure sterile whites.

### Semantic & Surface Rules
- **Canvas & Surface Layering**: Primary viewport canvas defaults to `#FBFBFC`. Modular sections, side panels, and inset groupings use `#F4F5F8`. Actionable data cards, analytic grids, and input containers maintain crisp `#FFFFFF` fill to ground the foreground.
- **Hairline Framing**: Structural boundaries use `#E2E4EB` or `#E8E5DF`. Grids and tabular matrices must never utilize heavy black or thick outlines; separators rely on exact 1px hairline treatments.
- **Market Data**: Positive trajectory leverages dignified British racing green (`#15803D`), while negative performance uses muted oxblood crimson (`#991B1B`), purposefully steering clear of electric or game-like neon hues.

## Typography

The typographic system communicates intellectual rigor and institutional permanence. Combining the refined geometry and tracking flexibility of Geist with the technical calibration of JetBrains Mono creates a balanced hierarchy tailored for high-stakes intelligence.

### Typographic Hierarchy & Character
- **Headlines & Narrative Units:** Set in Geist with tight negative tracking (`-0.015em` to `-0.035em`) to evoke tailored broadsheet layouts and high-end investor circulars.
- **Narrative Body Text:** Generous line heights (`1.6x` on `body-lg`) ensure fluid, publication-grade reading for thesis memos and quarterly commentary.
- **Monospace Data Architecture:** All numerical values, currency valuations, delta margins, leverage multiples, and index timestamps utilize tabular monospace settings with proportional numeric widths enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
- **Editorial Micro-Labels:** Section markers, category badges, and column headers use `label-caps` in uppercase JetBrains Mono with generous positive letter-spacing (`0.08em`) to anchor complex layouts.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is grounded in an asymmetrical, editorial broadsheet grid that prioritizes structural cohesion, clear content sequencing, and balanced negative space.

### Grid & Compositional Rhythm
- **Desktop (≥1280px):** 12-column adaptive layout with an expansive `2.5rem` outer margin. Multi-column financial analyses leverage a primary 8-column narrative/chart pane paired with a 4-column contextual ledger or briefing rail.
- **Tablet (768px – 1279px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margins. Complex financial tables switch to horizontal paning with pinned index columns.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with `1rem` margins. Multi-metric modules stack vertically into tactile cards, prioritizing the headline trajectory and key value before secondary metadata.

### Architectural Gap Strategy
Containers employ a `1px` structural hairline boundary system (`panel-gap: 1px`). Adjoining panels sit on an underlying `#E2E4EB` or `#E8E5DF` frame, producing razor-sharp, editorial dividers between modules without visual clutter or heavy borders.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system deliberately eschews fuzzy drop-shadows, synthetic neon blurs, and exaggerated three-dimensional skeuomorphism in favor of **Tonal Layering & Warm Hairline Framing**.

### Visual Depth Levels
- **Canvas Base (Elevation 0):** Uninterrupted `#FBFBFC` alabaster canvas. No shadow, purely foundational.
- **Recessed Modules & Secondary Shelves (Elevation 1):** `#F4F5F8` surface fill bounded by `1px solid #E2E4EB`. Creates tactile recessing for summary metrics, toolbars, and search bars.
- **Raised Analytical Cards (Elevation 2):** Pure `#FFFFFF` background encased in a crisp `1px solid #E2E4EB` outline. A minimal, diffuse ambient warmth (`0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 6px 16px -4px rgba(120, 53, 15, 0.02)`) provides physical separation from the canvas.
- **Overlays, Menus & Dialog Panels (Elevation 3):** Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface with an assertive boundary of `1px solid #0F172A / 12%`, grounded by a refined, tinted shadow (`0 12px 36px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(120, 53, 15, 0.03)`).
- **Interactive State Transitions:** Hover states avoid vertical translation ("pop-up" shifts). Instead, boundaries subtly shift from `#E2E4EB` to `#8D5B4C` (chestnut bronze), accompanied by a delicate background shift.

## Shapes

The geometric architecture relies on **Soft Tailored Radii (`roundedness: 1`)**, achieving an authoritative, institutional contour that avoids sterile sharp points while rejecting overly playful pill styling.

### Shape Distribution
- **Interactive Buttons & Inputs:** `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radius, imparting an engineered, calibrated demeanor.
- **Badges, Status Chips & Tickers:** `3px` to `4px` corner radius, harmonizing with monospaced character blocks.
- **Content Cards, Modal Surfaces & Group Containers:** `6px` to `8px` (`0.375rem` to `0.5rem`) corner radius for calm framing.
- **Segmented Selectors & Period Toggles:** Internal items strictly `3px`; parent wrapper `5px`. Circular and pill geometries are prohibited except for user profile avatars and unread counter badges.

## Components

### Buttons & Executive Actions
- **Primary Action:** Solid tailored navy `#0F172A` with `#FFFFFF` text. Border: `1px solid #0F172A`. On hover, subtle background shift to `#1E293B`.
- **Accent Action:** Rich saddle bronze `#78350F` with `#FFFFFF` text. Used for featured analytical tools, export mandates, and primary underwriting triggers.
- **Secondary / Outlined Action:** Background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`, border `1px solid #E2E4EB`. On hover: border transitions to `#8D5B4C` and background tints to `#FBFBFC`.
- **Ghost Action:** Background transparent, text `#475569`. Hover: background `#F4F5F8`, text `#0F172A`.

### Status Badges & Trajectory Chips
- **Institutional Milestone / Accent Chip:** Background `#FDF8F6`, text `#78350F`, hairline border `1px solid #E8D5CE`. Typography: `badge-sm`.
- **Positive Yield Marker:** Background `#F0FDF4`, text `#15803D`, border `1px solid #BBF7D0`. Tabular figures aligned with leading sign `+`.
- **Negative Drawdown Marker:** Background `#FEF2F2`, text `#991B1B`, border `1px solid #FECACA`. Tabular figures aligned with leading sign `-`.
- **Neutral Intelligence Tag:** Background `#F4F5F8`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid #E2E4EB`.

### Data Inputs & Search Selectors
- **Input Fields:** Pristine `#FFFFFF` background, `1px solid #E2E4EB` border, `4px` radius. Height fixed at `38px`. Text rendered in Geist 14px; placeholders in `#8A94A6`.
- **Active Focus State:** Border transitions crisply to `#0F172A` paired with a non-glowing outer rule: `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #0F172A`.
- **Monospace Financial Inputs:** Numerical amount entries use JetBrains Mono, anchored with a muted static currency indicator prefix (`USD`, `EUR`, `GBP`) in `#8A94A6`.

### Cards & Ledger Modules
- **Analytical Card:** Constructed on `#FFFFFF` with `1px solid #E2E4EB` framing. Header zones feature a dedicated `44px` height with bottom hairline border, containing uppercase category labels (`label-caps`) in `#8D5B4C`.
- **Tabular Data Tables:** Header rows styled in `#F4F5F8` with subtle border-bottom `1px solid #E2E4EB`. Table row height is fixed at `40px` for dense data density or `52px` for expanded portfolio views. Hover state is an understated `#F8F9FB`. Numeric columns align strictly right with monospace tabular numerals.

### Interactive Checkboxes & Segmented Controls
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** `16px` squared inputs with `3px` radius. Inactive: `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid #CBD5E1`. Selected: `#0F172A` fill with white geometric check icon.
- **Segmented Range Toggles (e.g., 1D, 1M, YTD, ALL):** Container styled with `#F4F5F8` and `1px solid #E2E4EB`. Active selection features `#FFFFFF` fill, `#0F172A` label, subtle hairline stroke, and zero vertical shift.
